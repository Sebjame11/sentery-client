Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sentery ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our sales intelligence platform (the "Service").

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, and password (stored securely as a hashed credential) when you create an account.
  • Work data: prospects, pipeline, notes, sequences, and other data you enter into the Service.
  • Connected accounts: if you connect Google Calendar or other integrations, we store the access tokens required to operate those integrations on your behalf.
  • Usage data: basic technical information such as browser type and device type for security and performance.

2. How We Use Information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Service.
  • To create calendar events and perform actions you explicitly enable through integrations.
  • To secure your account and prevent fraud or abuse.
  • To send transactional communications (e.g., account verification).

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure cloud infrastructure with encryption in transit and at rest. We do not sell your personal data. Access to your data is limited to authenticated users within your workspace.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. We share data only with service providers that help us operate the Service (hosting, email delivery) and only as needed, or when required by law.

5. Your Choices

  • You can export or delete your data at any time by contacting us.
  • You can disconnect third-party integrations from your settings page.
  • You can delete your account; we will remove your data within 30 days of a deletion request.

6. Contact

If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us via the support email listed in the application.
